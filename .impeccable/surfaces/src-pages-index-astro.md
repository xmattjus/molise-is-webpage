# Surface brief: landing page (`/`)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: residents and visitors of Molise deciding whether to install the app. Action: download from App Store or Google Play (official badges, working links). Proof: real app screenshots and real place/event names from them; no invented numbers, reviews or claims. Constraints: Ivory ground, Fraunces + Lexend, brand seeds + app Material containers, pin logo, Italian copy rewritten from true facts only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a village wall in Molise where the season's posters are pasted, and Molise Is is the poster on top that collects all the others. It refuses the app-landing template: split hero with a phone mockup, then a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory plaster wall as ground. Posters are full-colour paper sheets: Forest, Adriatic and Terra stocks, and the app containers (#84F8C3, #BDE9FF, #FFDBCF) as second-run paper. Heavy stacked Fraunces like a village print shop; Lexend caps for the info lines (where, when, how). Paste strips set diagonally across corners. Sheets hang slightly rotated with a soft paper shadow. Real screenshots are printed into the posters.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own region's wall (real places: Castelpetroso, Castel San Vincenzo, Agnone, Pettoranello), understands that the app gathers places, events and their own discoveries in one place instead of dozens of sources, believes it from the real screens, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header on the wall (pin logo + name, two links, download link). A full-width wall: the main Forest poster takes about 58% width on desktop, with "L'inatteso che ti aspetta" in Ivory Fraunces at display size, one line of Lexend, and a white info band at its foot holding both official badges (the primary action). Overlapping on the right, a tall poster printed with the real Home screenshot; behind it, smaller place and event posters with real names; a paste strip "Gratis su iPhone e Android". On mobile the main poster is full width and the screenshot poster peeks rotated below the badges.

FORM: Muri delle sagre, position 1 on my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by the user over the assigned direction). seed:e97ca07879c42c39. Signature interaction: in "Uno sopra l'altro", a scroll-driven stack where the Luoghi, Eventi and Scoperte posters peel off one after another, each revealing the next with its real screen; static side-by-side fallback without scroll timelines or with reduced motion. Motion grammar: posters are pasted once on load (settle from a slight lift), then stay still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
